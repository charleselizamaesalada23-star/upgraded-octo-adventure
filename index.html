About This Website

Safety

Privacy & Personal Safety

Privacy-first design

This personal website does not intentionally collect passwords, payment information, private documents, or other sensitive information.

Public information: Only publish information that you are comfortable sharing publicly.

Contact form: This standalone version does not store submissions in a database. Instead, the button prepares an email using your device's email application.

Personal safety: Avoid publishing your exact home address, private phone number, school schedule, passwords, authentication codes, or other information that could put you at risk.

Copyright: Only publish lyrics and other material that you created yourself or have permission to publish.
